# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 8/6/2026
**Last updated:** 8/6/2026

Coach Will ("we", "us") is operated by Jacob Dudley, PO Box 990148 Boston MA 02199. This
policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Contact for any privacy question or request: jdudley7060@gmail.com

## What we collect

**Account information.** When you sign in with Apple we receive a unique
identifier, and your name and email address if you choose to share them. If you
use Apple's Hide My Email, we only ever see the relay address.

**Health and fitness data.** Depending on which sources you connect:

- *Apple Health*: workouts, sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting
  heart rate, VO₂ max, steps, active energy, exercise minutes, body mass.
- *Oura*: sleep, readiness, heart rate variability, workouts.
- *Strava*: activities, including duration, distance, sport and heart rate.

We only read data. We never write to Apple Health, and we never post to Strava.

**What you tell us.** Your training goal, any notes you add, and the messages you
send to the coach.

**Technical data.** Server logs containing IP address, timestamps and error
diagnostics, retained for security and debugging.

## How we use it

- To generate your daily readiness brief and training prescription.
- To answer your questions in the coach chat.
- To show your training history and compliance over time.
- To operate, secure and debug the service.

**We do not use your health data for advertising. We do not sell it. We do not
share it with data brokers.** Apple's HealthKit terms prohibit this and so do we.

## Automated processing and AI

Your health data and messages are sent to **Anthropic PBC** to generate coaching
responses. Anthropic processes this on our behalf as a service provider and does
not use it to train models. See Anthropic's privacy policy at
anthropic.com/privacy.

The coach's output is generated automatically. It is not reviewed by a human
before you see it.

We share with these providers only what is needed to run the service. We may also

If the business is sold or merged, your data may transfer to the acquirer. You
will be notified before that happens.

## Where it is stored and for how long

Data is stored in the United States. Provider access tokens are encrypted at
rest. All traffic is encrypted in transit.

We keep your data while your account is open. If you delete your account, we
remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep records to
comply with law. Backups are purged on their normal rotation.

## Your rights

You may, at any time:

- **Access** the data we hold about you.
- **Export** it in a machine-readable format.
- **Correct** anything inaccurate.
- **Delete** your account and data, from within the app.
- **Disconnect** any provider, which stops further collection from that source.
- **Withdraw consent** for health data by revoking access in iOS Settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under the GDPR (EU
and UK) or the CCPA/CPRA (California), including the right to object to
processing and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising a right.
We do not sell personal information as those laws define it.

To exercise any of these, email jdudley7060@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

## Children

Coach Will is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data
from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will
delete it.

## Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, scoped access tokens, and least
privilege for infrastructure access. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot
guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you
and the relevant regulator as required by law.

## Changes

We will post any change here and update the date above. For material changes we
will notify you in the app before they take effect.
